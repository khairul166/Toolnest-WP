How to Convert Image Formats in Your Browser

  1. Step 1 — Choose Output Format & Quality: Select whether you want to export your files as JPEG (.jpg), WebP (.webp), or lossless PNG (.png), and set the quality slider.
  2. Step 2 — Drop Your Images (Including iPhone HEIC): Drag and drop one or multiple JPG, PNG, WebP, or HEIC/HEIF files into the upload zone.
  3. Step 3 — Compare Sizes & Export ZIP: Review the original vs. converted file sizes and click Download All as .ZIP or save individual files.

Why Convert Between HEIC, WebP, PNG, and JPG Locally?

Modern smartphones and web browsers use specialized image formats that are not always compatible with older government job portals, university admission forms, or desktop software. Apple iPhones capture photos in HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) by default, while websites serve graphics in Google's WebP format. ToolNest's Image Format Converter bridges all of these formats right inside your browser without uploading private photos to any server.
