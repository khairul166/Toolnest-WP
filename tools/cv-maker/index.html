      How to Build & Download Your CV

      1. Step 1 — Enter Your Personal & Contact Info: Fill in your name, professional title, email, phone number, location, skills, and a concise career summary.
      2. Step 2 — Add Experience, Education & Projects: Click the Add Experience, Add Education, Add Certification, and Add Project buttons to list your background.
      3. Step 3 — Choose a Template & Save as PDF: Pick one of the three resume styles and click Print / Save PDF to export a clean A4 resume using your browser's print dialog.
